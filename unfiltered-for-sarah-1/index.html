unfiltered for Sarah #1

i guess i am writing here.  a new page or something, for you.  

you're sleeping now - or i hope you are.  i'm not writing on my phone because it takes too much effort to tap each letter and maybe a lot of words are coming.  i suppose i should write about her.  i suppose you deserve to know.  

it's very possible i'm being unfair.  it's possible i'm being unfair to you, unfair to her, and i don't know.  i'm just doing my best.  josh has been when i am and he helps me keep my reality clearer.  i wish you could just talk to him and see what he says - because my experience is in this fish bow and it's so hard - so often.  and i'm a fool. i dream and i hope and i do all the things that an enlightened person isn't supposed to do. i try not to - and i'm ok with dreaming and hoping and even enjoying my illusions.  you often feel like an illusion because how could you be real?  i'm losing the mark and rambling. 

my relationship hurts me.  it drains me.  it takes from me in ways that are hard to explain and i hold compassion for the person with the need that wishes to tell me stories about myself. who has a dysregulated nervous system and is trying to manage it but apt to confuse me for the source of their state or blame me for not helping alieviate that state. i helped so long in so many ways.  this isn't my house.  it's her house.  eventually we'll spend down our savings from the house that was sold.  it's a few months out and she's telling me not to get coffees while i'm out and that she's the sole bread winner.  she gets fomo and it hurts her heart to be left out.  baby lizards find their way in the house and she tries to save them.  she has a little area in the back yard where the anoles gather.  they have names and personalities.  she becomes light and free and yeah.  

i have a lot of poetry i could send you - with my stories of the now or me trying to process current emotions.  but the truth is - her nervous system separates her from the world and the world becomes aggressive and dysregulating and she attaches motive.  she forgets we're on the same side.  she tells me i'm her enemy.  she tells me i'm trying to hurt her.  she tells me all kinds of bad things about myself.  she likes it when i cook for her and is really complimentary.  she likes it when i do things for her.  i like to do things for people.  i'm a youngest child.  intermittent reinforcement breaks my heart.  please don't break my heart.  

this is probably a lot to read in the morning.  i wonder if you've read the poetry book i sent you.  i wonder if you wonder if i still have some entanglements.  i'm barely entangled with myself dear.  or maybe i'm highly entangled with myself.  i'm tired but i want to keep writing because it feels good to write with you in mind.  to think that you may read these words and that i may - for a moment - sit in the sunshine of your empathy.  things are dark for me and i'm trying to crawl out on my own. i'm trying to spread love in the world. i'm trying to put out what i need. i'm trying to live authentically. i'm trying not to let my brain take me away from the people that love me. i'm a lot dear.  i'm sorry.  and writing i'm sorry hurts more than anything else because i really am.  

when i was diagnosed with AS, I felt so guilty.  I felt so sad.  My body had already started changing when she and I met and I was only beautiful for a short time with her.  she let me know it.  she was different back then.  i say that but it hurts to read my spirals back then.  she walked away from herself when we met because I was a ray of sunlight in a dark dark world and she ran towards it.  you're a ray of sunlight too - the first real light like you've i've seen in a while, and i'm desperately trying not to run towards you.  

i'm not broken. i'm not small.  i'm not pacified - i'm just trying to survive and be kind.  i'm trying to preserve my energy and learning to disengage and de-enmesh.  but she's such a kind person and i know her when she is light and free and the world isn't stealing her. when the world isn't so dysregulating.  i have empathy for that.  but i'm tired of being treated like an enemy.  i'm tired of being forgotten and told stories about myself that aren't true.  i'm not a saint.  i have participated in toxic dynamics and believed stories about myself. i've been cast and played those parts.  i don't hate her or even dislike her, 

I wish she could have a friend like you. she would make you matcha or some other kind of tea and bake something.  she's good at making drinks.  she's very sweet and thoughtful.  she notices Josh doesn't do ice in his drinks and makes sure that Josh's drink doesn't have ice but instead cools it by pouring it through ice trying to get it at the right temperature.  i wrote some poems and put them on a website and she printed it out.  the website went away - and one day she lost the poetry.  she went into a deep depression for months.  she is cruel to that girl in her mind and judges her harshly and invalidates her.  

even in the ambiguity that you may actually - i don't know - connect with me on an intimate level - so other cosmic level - the thing that is most alluring is just - being taken as i am.  that i have no money - no assets - that i can do nothing for you other than just being me - and that it may be enough.  even my pain riddled bent body somehow being enough.  i believe i am worthy of just being loved for who i am - but it's an act of faith.

i don't know what i'm trying to say.  a few nights ago a black cat came by the front door.  a black cat sometimes comes but the front door is weird. it was peeking around and such.  i went outside to check the mail (which is when I found out the mailbox was kicked over) and the black cat came back a few seconds later after i closed the door (cameras are cool).   So I got some food for the black cat - and put it on a paper plate and took it outside.  A lizard fell from somewhere and landed on the ground, lifeless.  I tried to see if it was alive and it wasn't.  i knew it would hurt her to find it but I didn't know what to do.  I saw a planter and was going to put it under the planter.  It was in bad shape.  I put it under the planter and i thought about the poor little buddy suffering in the dark under there - or being eaten by the cat - so the act of compassion found a violence.   Geckos have this thing where their tails have nerves in them and when detached the automatically wiggle. It's a defense mechanism.  It started wiggling but I had already taken an action.  So I followed through and followed through - my heart aching with each attempt while the tail in its involuntary cycle kept moving.  I then realized what was happening and stopped.  Put the planter over it and took a moment - or a few moments to sit in the sorrow of it and to forgive myself - and left the food for the black cat.   Later I'd check online to make sure I understood things properly about the tail.  I don't know if it was all an empty act, a mercy, or violence.  It is something that happened.  It's a true story and I'm not proud of it but also not ashamed.  that version of me did their best in that moment and was only trying to act in compassion and kindness.  

Should I have left the cat be to find its own food?  I've never seen the cat again.  Then the lizard would not have fallen and perhaps it would have lived - or that it had already passed.  If I had not stepped through that door then - what would have happened?  But I did and I made a decision.  The consequences of what happened don't define me or even weigh on me.  I have compassion for that foolish heart that wanted to make things better and messed things up.  

And yes, this is a lot.  Yes, something happened tonight.  She was playing a game and getting to a stopping point. In the meatime, I was finishing up a text to you.  She may have finished early but saw me on my phone.  I scrolled and read after the text so maybe she didn't know.  She kept playing the game.  Then she asks if I'm done texting and I say yes.  And tries to make me culpable for her waiting on me to finish texting when I had for a while.  We didn't communicate well.  I was waiting for her to say when she got to a stopping point and she was waiting on me to say when I finished texting.  I was fine with the shape of the night, ready to watch the show - but she was unhappy that I had not been texting or had not told her when i finished.  her dysregulation led to accusations about not wanting to spend time with her and those accusations made me not want to spend time with her.  

I want her to have everything so that when I leave - she has everything.  I don't want anything.  I just want to be free.  I wish she could just be a friend to me - or family - and she tries so hard to be - but she loses sight of me and this desert continues.  

So this is an unfiltered me.  a collection of thoughts and moments.  i like you.  i don't like the idea of you. i don't like what you can or cannot offer me.  i just like you - but i forget sometimes about what pressure cooker i'm in. i forget how much capacity she still has to hurt me and i her. i try to keep myself regulated.  i try to stay vigilant.  i try to be helpful when asked.  i do.  she's not bad.  she isn't.  i really do wish she had a friend like you.  i'm so glad she has a friend like Josh. I wish she had more friends.  I wish she had someone to love her the way she wants/needs to be loved.  I feel like if I would just get out of the way - I would.  But I have no where to go.  I could return to a floor and a blanket and a pillow - as I was so many years ago.  

So I could drown in what bliss you offer, but the empathy of your being is what calls to me. it's what rips at my heart.  the eyes that see some unmistakableness in me and recognizes me and wants nothing more than for me to be whole and happy - without compromising your own equillibrium.  I'm trying.  I'm working so hard.  

Tomorrow she wants to go to leaf after my infusion.  She doesn't want me to invite you or Josh and wants it to just be us.  I'm going to be in a haze of the spravato treatment - likely hungry - likely - still - a little melancholy.  but even now - i hope that the empty chair across the table can be her when you, I, and Josh cross paths.  I do.  I want her to belong. I want her to feel safe.  I want her to have the kind of love you and Josh embody.  But I want that for me too.  I need that for me.

I'm sure there's more, but I'm so tired... that message.  about the hug and falling into a state of equillibrium.. it's beautiful. the whole of the response is beautiful.  even if all of this is too much, and that would be rightfully so, i have cherished these moments - for exactly as they are.  i do not need you to rescue me. that isn't your responsibility.  i don't know why i'm in your life or if there's any reason at all.  i just know that i like chatting with you and this now - this current period - despite all that is - is a beautiful time.  

tomorrow morning i'm going to help her schedule her foot mri for her hurt foot - and maybe get feedback from the disability attorney - then the spravato infusion and then Leaf and then probably crash.  but when I do.  i feel your hug.  

callie and marie - my sister girls are fighting.  marie is my heart - she curls up beside me each night - like a little spoon - until i fall asleep - purring against my chest - and sits vigil over me and waits fo rme to stir and repeat the same process.  i'm so blessed.  and callie is the most trusting cat... i broke them up.. .marie was on the chase.  for some reason.  sometimes i think they are souls play acting.  

I hope you sleep well.  I'm going to send this to you now.  I'm going to try to warn you against reading it.  this will likely disappear dear.  i have so much more i could say but i'm so tired and it's amost tomorrow.
